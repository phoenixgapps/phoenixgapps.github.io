Privacy Policy

Smart Subtitle Translator (GR) · Effective 2 November 2026

Smart Subtitle Translator (GR) ("the app") is made by [DEVELOPER NAME]. It translates English subtitle files (.srt) into Greek. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • The translation happens entirely on your phone. Your subtitle files are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app has no accounts, and we don't collect, receive or sell any of your personal information.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob while it translates, when your phone is online. Google may collect some information for those ads, as explained below.

Your subtitle files

The app only opens the files you choose with Android's file picker, and saves the Greek files where you choose. The translator is built into the app, so the text of your subtitles stays on your phone. The app works without an internet connection.

History

So you can open a translation again later, the app keeps each file you translate (the English, the Greek and any lines you fixed, with the file's name and the date) in its own private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't sent to us. You can remove any file from the History tab, or clear it all; uninstalling the app deletes it too. If Android's backup to your Google account is turned on for your phone, Android may include this history in that backup, as it does for other apps; you can turn this off in your phone's settings.

Ads

The app is free to use and is supported by ads, which come from Google AdMob. Ads are shown only while the app is translating, and only when your phone is online. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out;
  • information about your device and the app (model, Android version, language), and how you interact with the ads;
  • diagnostic information, such as how well the ads load.

This information goes to Google, not to us. Google handles it under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads can be personalised, and you can say no. Until you've answered, nothing is measured for ads.
  • Changing your mind. Where the consent form applies, tap the ⓘ button at the top of the app, then Ad privacy settings, to see the form again and change your answer.
  • Your advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Ads personalisation. You can manage how Google personalises ads at myadcenter.google.com.

Links to other websites

The app's Credits screen links to the websites of the projects it's built on (such as the translation model's page). They open in your browser and have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your files and history stay in Android's protected app storage on your phone. Information the ads SDK sends to Google is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy
